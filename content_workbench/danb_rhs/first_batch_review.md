## How to review

Use the [review checklist](../../docs/DANB_RHS_QUESTION_REVIEW_CHECKLIST.md) and
[approval workflow](../../docs/DANB_RHS_CONTENT_APPROVAL_WORKFLOW.md).
Open each cited section personally. The knowledge objectives, difficulty
rationales and distractor misconceptions below are **editorial hypotheses for
review**, not observations about learners and not clinical sign-off. Source
locators support the rule or distinction; misconceptions are the editor's
inferences about why an alternative is wrong, not quotations from the source.

Source dates, institutions, currency limits and access details are retained in
[source_register.md](source_register.md) and in each question's references.
The cited clinical pages were re-read on 2026-09-11 for this revision. Full
2026/2024 JADA papers were not read; PT-002 and PT-010 deliberately stay within
the public ADA summary and carry explicit expert-review notes. No local-law
or device-specific protocol is inferred. The four missing original technical
slots still need accessible anatomy/projection/error-correction references.

Fingerprints use **`sha256-canonical-json-v1`**, generated by the existing
`computeContentFingerprint(Question)` in `tool/candidate_question_validator.dart`.
The current `--report` CLI checks status but does not print these hashes; they
were obtained using that function through a temporary Dart probe, removed
after use. No hash algorithm or schema was changed.

Next step: a qualified human reviews each exact version below, checks the
sources and distractors, and personally enters the real identity, date,
decision and matching fingerprint through the existing workflow. A request for
revision remains appropriate where the listed limitations cannot be resolved.
