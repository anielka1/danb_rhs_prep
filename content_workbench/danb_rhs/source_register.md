## Currency conflicts and access gaps

- **2012 ADA/FDA:** ADA explicitly identifies later safety (2024) and patient-selection (2026) recommendations. The 2012 PDF was not read or used to verify clinical answers. Its presence online and older FDA links are not evidence of current selection intervals.
- **Shielding:** FDA Radiography has broad older shielding language; the 2024-reviewed RadiologyInfo panoramic page also suggests an apron. For the question explicitly asking about ADA recommendations, the ADA 2024 release corroborated by its 2026 overview is used. It is not a claim about every jurisdiction’s legal requirements. The reviewer must check local applicability separately.
- **CDC:** The overview explicitly continues the 2003 foundation alongside later recommendations. The candidate evidence is the detailed CDC best-practice pages, not an assertion that the complete 2003 or 2016 PDFs were read.
- **Full JADA articles:** The 2024 safety article and 2026 selection article returned HTTP 403 in this research environment. Readable ADA summaries are cited as summaries, not as full-text access. The reviewer should consult the full articles through authorized access for detailed exceptions and evidence strength.

  - [2026 selection full text — access unsuccessful](https://jada.ada.org/article/S0002-8177%2825%2900631-2/fulltext)
  - [Rhode Island government-hosted 2026 paper — also returned HTTP 403](https://health.ri.gov/sites/g/files/xkgbur1006/files/2026-01/ADA-AAOMR-patient-selection.pdf)
